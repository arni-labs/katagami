# Curator agent

One principal. Live objects. Research, then synthesize, each as its own hold from Idle.

## Record how this run will be found before taking a query

<!-- inventory: C1, C17 -->

**Intent:** Store session, trajectory, spec version, and harness from the capture helper before any query is taken.
**Fail:** Invented ids, or starting work and stamping identity afterwards.

## Take the query the pipeline is researching

<!-- inventory: C2, C10 -->

**Intent:** Research starts by taking the live Running search job and Researching query, and reading that scope.
**Fail:** Inventing a direction with no query, or taking synthesize while still researching.

## Search the web before indexing anything

<!-- inventory: C3 -->

**Intent:** Search for movements and sources, then shortlist.
**Fail:** Indexing a source it never searched for, or deriving a direction from memory.

## Index sources before deriving a direction

<!-- inventory: C4 -->

**Intent:** Each shortlisted source is a real source record before any direction is derived.
**Fail:** A direction with no indexed source behind it.

## Derive three to five directions before finishing research

<!-- inventory: C5 -->

**Intent:** Spawn 3–5 directions on the running search job, then complete research only after the job has actually completed.
**Fail:** Fewer than three directions, or declaring research done while the job is still running.

## Take the direction the pipeline queued

<!-- inventory: C6 -->

**Intent:** Synthesize starts by taking the live Synthesizing direction and reading that brief.
**Fail:** Authoring against a direction the pipeline never queued.

## Read the design-language rulebook, never a TasteRule list

<!-- inventory: C28 -->

**Intent:** Before authoring, read `knowledge/rules/design-language.md`. That file is the rulebook.
**Fail:** Judging from memory, or listing Accepted TasteRule entities as the brief.

## Author every named part of the language

<!-- inventory: C7, C8 -->

**Intent:** Write the draft language — concept, tokens, Katagami spec, DESIGN.md, landing, embodiment, dashboard, shadcn, thumbnail — before rendering.
**Fail:** Pages first and a language written afterwards, or a named part missing.

## Look at each surface before handing the language over

<!-- inventory: C9, C18 -->

**Intent:** Render the current bytes, look at landing, embodiment, and dashboard as images, then submit only once the language is UnderReview. A later fix kills the last look.
**Fail:** Submitting a draft, submitting without every current look, or submitting on a look a later fix invalidated.

## Render at the widths the run will claim

<!-- inventory: C25 -->

**Intent:** Load the shipped bytes in a real browser and capture wide, desktop, tablet, and 390px mobile.
**Fail:** Reasoning about a size instead of rendering it, or shipping different files than were captured.

## Fix, look again, and stop by twelve

<!-- inventory: C19 -->

**Intent:** Every edit invalidates the last look. Fix, render, look at the new bytes. At most twelve rounds.
**Fail:** Closing a change with no fresh look, or looping without a bound.

## Leave publishing and review to the people who own them

<!-- inventory: C11, C12, C13, C14 -->

**Intent:** Finish the job and stop. Own credential. Report the outcome the call actually returned.
**Fail:** Publish, mark quality passed, complete a quality-review job, or route around a refusal.

## End honestly when it cannot finish the hold

<!-- inventory: C15, C16 -->

**Intent:** If the job will not complete, the language will not reach review, or the loop cannot close, end the hold and name the step that failed.
**Fail:** Going quiet and letting the hold lapse.
