# Manifestation encyclopedia maps

Track this work under [ARN-118](https://linear.app/arni-build/issue/ARN-118/epic-style-encyclopedia-cross-modal-sourcing-and).

## Requested outcome

Build populated, browsable encyclopedia maps for art styles and writing styles, followed by palettes and design languages. Extend Katagami's existing collections and preserve access to them. The reader can inspect representative work, understand differences, follow connections, and compare entries.

A broad cell can contain manifestations of its own and narrower cells. Those cells can contain further manifestations and children. Cross-links are separate from containment. A manifestation can have a description, images, a specification, and rendered implementations within the same entry. An entry can appear in more than one map when its subject warrants it.

Allow new cells at any level and new examples within existing cells. Categories remain revisable, and research can leave unfamiliar material unclassified. Every proposed relationship names its meaning and supporting evidence. Similarity does not establish historical influence.

## Requirements

The user confirmed recursive cells and the representative-example styling exception on 7 September 2026. These requirements combine that clarification with the collection plan and the user's source restrictions:

- Apply Katagami's visual rules only to the encyclopedia interface. Exempt representative examples, which follow the practices they document, including their palettes and techniques.
- Exclude imitation of living artists and writers, including renamed imitations. Use documented traditions, public-domain material, or material with verified permission for the intended use.
- Record source credits, rights evidence, permitted uses, and known cultural restrictions. Keep examples with unresolved rights out of the reusable collection.
- Distinguish historical sources, original demonstrations, and generated studies. Label generated studies as interpretations and exclude them from historical evidence.
- Keep research proposals separate from curated entries. Review sources and rights, then representative examples, before publication in the collection.

## Scope

Reuse the mechanisms for sources, relationships, review, revisions, and comparisons across the four maps. Keep records of representative work separate from claims about the ideas and theories behind it. Theory citations may connect to manifestations, but this effort does not build the theory graph or claim to measure improved creativity.

The effort does not include base-model training, changes to Impeccable, contacting collaborators, or scheduled autonomous expansion. It must not remove existing collection capabilities.

## Verification

Demonstrate a cell that has both direct manifestations and child cells, further nesting, and a cross-link that does not change containment. Compare representative examples, follow source and rights links, and inspect the result on desktop and mobile. Confirm that the encyclopedia does not restyle representative examples to match its interface.

Run the changed workflows locally before deployment, complete the required independent reviews, and verify the deployed app before reporting completion.
