Morning-room in a house in Cadogan Place. The room is comfortable, and rather too full of flowers.

[Parker is arranging letters upon a tray. Lady Caroline enters.]

CAROLINE. Are all of those for me, Parker?

PARKER. Three of them, my lady. The rest are bills. I have put them where Mr. Rupert will see them, and where he will not be obliged to open them.

CAROLINE. That was very thoughtful of you. Bills always know perfectly well where they are wanted. [Parker goes out.] My nephew has been in the country since Tuesday. That is a dangerous quantity of fresh air for a young man with expectations.

[Enter Rupert.]

RUPERT. Aunt Caroline! How well you are looking. I don’t think I have ever seen you look so well, and I have seen you every Sunday since Easter.

CAROLINE. I always look well when I am displeased. Sit down. I suppose you have come up to town to tell me about the mare.

RUPERT. [Sitting.] I had not intended to mention her at all.

CAROLINE. I have heard about the mare. I have also heard about the second one. I am told there is a third in the stables at this moment, but I don’t believe everything I am told, and I have made a particular point of not believing that.

RUPERT. It was an economy. One cannot hunt upon a single animal, and hunting is a duty in the country.

CAROLINE. An economy is simply an expense that one has enjoyed. My dear boy, you have been extremely young, and that costs a great deal more than being extravagant.

RUPERT. Then I shall be useful instead. I have thought of standing for Parliament.

CAROLINE. Useful! On which side?

RUPERT. I had not considered that. It seemed to me a detail.

CAROLINE. How thoroughly modern of you. Your uncle Gerald was defeated at Warwick. It was the one thing he ever did with conviction, and the family has been grateful to him ever since.

RUPERT. Then there is Miss Loftus. She has thirty thousand pounds, and she is very fond of me.

CAROLINE. She has thirty thousand pounds and no conversation.

RUPERT. She talks about me for hours together.

CAROLINE. That is not conversation. That is charity.

[Enter Parker.]

PARKER. Miss Loftus, my lady. She has come about the tea.

CAROLINE. Already! Show her in. [Parker goes out.] Rupert, stand up. And say nothing whatever about the stables.

RUPERT. Which stables?

CAROLINE. That is very much better.
