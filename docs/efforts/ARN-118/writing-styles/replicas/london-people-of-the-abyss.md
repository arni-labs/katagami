I bought a coat for eightpence in Scotland Road, and in it I went down to the dock gates at Sandon Basin and stood in the pen with the other men from half-past six. Understand, before anything else, that I could go home. That is the whole distance between me and the four hundred who stood there with me, and I do not intend to pretend it away.

They call it the stand, and the word is exact. You stand. The gate is shut, and the men pack against it in a half-circle five and six deep, and the ones at the back climb the wall or the bollards so that their faces can be seen. At eight o'clock a gaffer comes out with a book, and looks the half-circle over as a man looks over horses, and calls perhaps thirty names, and perhaps forty. Then the gate shuts again. The rest wait for the one o'clock call, and if there is none they go away.

The pay, when a man is taken on, is fivepence the hour, with a shilling extra for a night turn. The register kept by the dock board gives twenty-seven thousand men holding tallies on this waterfront. The berths on any average morning are near eleven thousand. Set the two figures together and you have the arithmetic of the pen: sixteen thousand men are required to be at the gate at half-past six in order that they may be told they are not wanted at eight.

I stood next to a man of about fifty who had been a ship's fireman and could not get a ship.

"They knows me 'ere, an' that's agin me," he said. "A young 'un can do the four hours an' then do four more. I can do the four. So the gaffer takes the young 'un. Only what's a man to do at fifty? I've no trade but shovellin', an' me shovellin' days is done, an' there's nowhere I can go an' say I'm forty."

At eight he was not called, and neither was I, and we walked up together as far as the Prince's Road.

There is the case. A city which needs sixteen thousand men to be idle at its gate every morning, so that it may be sure of eleven thousand at work behind it, is keeping those men as it keeps its cranes and its chains, and is paying for them only on the days it uses them.
