The desk

Everything registers in its place

Long reading falls apart when the pieces scatter. Kento holds the queue, the place, and the margins on one desk — so a twelve-part essay reads like one book.

The queue

Pin a season of installments in reading order. Kento meters the backlog — 40 minutes tonight, the finale by Friday.

The place

Your line, not just your page. Come back after three weeks and the desk reopens mid-sentence, with a two-line recap above it.

The margins

Press a note into the margin as you read. Notes stack into a private commonplace book, indexed by serial and author.

How it works

Three passes, like a print

1

Set the block

Choose a serial and Kento lays the whole run on your desk — every installment, its length, and where the season pauses.

2

Pull the proof

Read in a quiet, typeset page. The desk keeps your exact line and folds in recaps when a gap gets long.

3

Register the color

Margins, highlights, and finished marks press onto the run. When the finale lands, your whole reading is one bound record.
